# Shield Insurance – Business Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Shield Insurance's business performance, customer distribution, premium revenue, sales-channel contribution and age-group patterns.

The project transforms raw insurance datasets into meaningful business insights to support performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall customer base and premium revenue.
- Track daily customer and revenue growth.
- Analyze monthly policy sales trends.
- Evaluate sales-channel performance and contribution.
- Understand customer distribution and revenue across age groups.
- Explore city-wise business performance and policy preferences.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Business Overview
- Total Customers and Total Revenue.
- Daily Customer and Revenue Growth Rates.
- Monthly Revenue and Customer Trends.
- Monthly Policy Sales and MoM Growth.
- City-wise Customer and Revenue Distribution.
- Age-group Customer and Revenue Segmentation.

### 2. Sales Mode Analysis
- Customer and Revenue Contribution by Sales Mode.
- Sales Mode Distribution using Donut Charts.
- Monthly Revenue Trends across four channels:
  - Offline-Agent
  - Offline-Direct
  - Online-App
  - Online-Website

### 3. Age Group Analysis
- Policy Preference by Age Group.
- Customer Distribution across Age Bands.
- Average Settlement Percentage by Age Group.
- Sales Mode Preference across Age Groups.
- Monthly Customer Trends by Age Group.
- Revenue Contribution and Average Revenue per Customer.

## Key Business Insights
- **26,841 customers** and approximately **₹989.25M** in total premium revenue.
- Offline-Agent contributes **55.41% of customers** and **55.67% of revenue**, making it the leading sales channel.
- Delhi NCR accounts for the largest customer share at **41.01%**.
- The 36–45 age group represents the largest customer segment, contributing **33.40% of total revenue**.
- The Above 55 age group records the highest average revenue per customer at **₹76,967.52**.
- April 2023 policy transaction volume declined by **41.41%** compared with March 2023.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization
- DAX – Measures and KPI calculations

## Dataset Overview
The project uses five datasets:
- `fact_premiums.csv`
- `dim_customer.csv`
- `dim_date.csv`
- `dim_policies.csv`
- `fact_settlements.csv`

**Reporting Period:** November 2022 – April 2023

## Key Metrics
- Total Customers
- Total Premium Revenue
- Daily Customer Growth Rate
- Daily Revenue Growth Rate
- Monthly Policy Sales Growth
- Customer and Revenue Contribution
- Average Revenue per Customer

## Project Outcome
Developed an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard that brings together customer, revenue, channel and age-group analysis, making insurance business performance easier to monitor and interpret.
